# Literature and citation verification

The manuscript cites fourteen relevant works. DOI metadata were retrieved through DOI content negotiation with publisher-deposited records on 2026-10-29. Author order, title, year, journal, volume, issue, and pagination were checked where supplied. Full subtitles for Iyengar–Sood–Lelkes and Schaffner–Luks were checked against the publication pages because their deposited title fields omit them. Kahan et al. was checked against the Stanford Law Review article, which has no DOI recorded here. `citations.csv` records the source for each entry.

The bibliography is focused on sources used in the manuscript rather than carrying the previous unrelated bibliography collection. Previous entries remain in Git history. The literature does not substitute for the study's own estimates, and no citation is used to turn a null result into evidence of equivalence.
